=============================================================

CONTEXT
- The Render relay works end to end: Retell -> https://swift-w7bb.onrender.com (relay/app.py) -> relay/client.py
  on the laptop -> agent.server on :8080 -> Qwen (agent/llm.py). Commit c503e42. Webhook arrived, status needs_help.
- First real demo call to Eric's phone went through all 13 turns, but the saved record was wrong.
- Root trigger: Eric's answers drifted one question behind the agent (said "Yep" to the address question, then the
  rehearsal line "Yes, that's right" to the people question). That's a demo-rehearsal issue, but the agent's handling
  of it exposed four real weaknesses below. A real resident (or a judge) will trigger them too.

TRANSCRIPT (agent / resident)
  A: ...Am I speaking with someone at 1586 Eagle Trail Drive?
  R: Yep.
  A: Got it, thanks for confirming. How many people are at the home right now...?
  R: Yes. That's right.
  A: Thank you for confirming that. Is there anyone there who can't leave on their own...? If it's a wheelchair, does it have a motor?
  R: Yes.
  A: Thank you for sharing that information. Is there water inside the home right now?
  R: Yes. Me and my mom. My mom uses a power wheelchair.
  A: I hear you're both inside with water present. If neighbors need a ride out, do you have a high-clearance truck, a boat, or free seats...?
  R: No.
  A: ...Let me read that back: people at the home not confirmed; wheelchair needs help leaving because of manual; water is inside. Is that right?
  R: No. I do not.
  A: I hear you're not sure about the read-back. I've recorded that you need help leaving. No responder has been assigned yet. ...Goodbye.

RECORDED: people=None, cant_leave_alone=[{who:"wheelchair", need:"manual"}], water_inside=True
HOUSE CARD: "? people. Can't leave alone: wheelchair: manual. Water inside. Language: en."
EXPECTED (from what was actually said overall): people=2, cant_leave_alone=[{who:"mom", need:"power wheelchair"}].

ISSUES (priority order)
1. Invented facts from the agent's own question (WORST — puts false info on a rescue card).
   A bare "Yes." to the cant_leave question became {who:"wheelchair", need:"manual"} — words from the question, not
   the resident. Fix: in agent/llm.py extract(), drop any person whose who/need doesn't appear (or isn't clearly
   implied) in the resident's utterance; a bare yes/no to cant_leave should set a "yes, details unknown" flag and
   trigger a follow-up ("Who is it, and what do they need?"), never a fabricated person. Also tighten the SYSTEM /
   per-step prompt: "Only use words the resident said; never copy examples from the question."

2. No re-ask when the answer doesn't fit the question.
   "Yes. That's right." to "How many people?" got "Thank you for confirming" and moved on with people=None.
   Fix: in agent/script.py advance(), if the step's required field is still empty after extraction, re-ask once
   (there's already a re-ask path for empty replies — see test_empty_reply_reasks_once; extend it to "reply had no
   usable value for this step"). The ack should not claim to have understood when nothing was extracted.

3. Extraction is scoped to the current step only, so volunteered info is lost.
   "Yes. Me and my mom. My mom uses a power wheelchair." (answered during water_inside) lost people=2 and the
   power wheelchair. Fix: let extract() also return out-of-step fields when clearly stated (people count,
   cant_leave persons), and have _merge() fill them only if currently empty. Watch the "ALL keys required" schema
   gotcha in agent/llm.py (the model skips optional keys) — use nullable values, not optional keys.

4. "No" to the read-back doesn't lead to a correction.
   Agent closed with "I've recorded that you need help leaving" after the resident said the read-back was wrong.
   Fix: on a negative readback, ask "What should I fix?", extract against all fields, merge, read back once more;
   only then close. Cap at one correction round to keep calls short. (test_correction_keeps_cant_leave exists —
   build on it.)
